Research

My research interests lie in 3D vision and spatial intelligence, with a focus on dynamic scene representation, world models, and 3D/4D reconstruction and generation. I am particularly excited about building 3D-native multimodal foundation models and agents that can represent, reason about, and interact with dynamic scenes across space and time, toward more capable systems for real-world understanding, generation, and embodied interaction.
