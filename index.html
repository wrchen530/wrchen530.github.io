About

Hi! I am a third-year PhD student at TU Munich and University of Oxford through ELLIS, advised by Daniel Cremers and Andrea Vedaldi. I also work closely with Chuanxia Zheng and Nikita Araslanov. I am currently a Research Scientist Intern at Meta Reality Labs Research, and was previously a student researcher at Google XR.

Before my PhD, I received my Master's degree from ETH Zurich, advised by Marc Pollefeys, and worked on 3D vision projects at Microsoft Spatial AI Lab. Earlier, I obtained my Bachelor's degree from CUHK and interned at SenseTime Research.

Research

My research spans 3D/4D reconstruction & generation, world models, and embodied AI: learning how dynamic worlds look, move, and evolve, so that agents can predict and act in them.

arXiv / Project Page / Code (coming soon)
